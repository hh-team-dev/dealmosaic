## 1. Decision in one minute

Use **Our Business 01 as the opportunity map**, not as a verified integration manual. Keep its network-first distribution, existing infrastructure, relevant cross-selling and attention to attribution. Add Our Business 02's evidence, comparable conditions, contractual boundaries and narrow-pilot discipline. Do not rebuild the working customer experience just to resemble either document.

**CPC gets first investigation priority, not fictional activation.** Execution priority is the easiest **eligible, approved, useful and zero-upfront-cost** route. An existing approved CPS promotion can be closer to the first payment than an unapproved CPC programme. An advertiser still has to agree to pay; a button, click counter or affiliate-network account cannot create that agreement.

The site is **not leak-proof, not connected to automatic room-rate APIs, and not guaranteed to earn**. The current stronger parts are comparison accuracy and operating safeguards—not proven market leadership or giant-platform revenue. Physical-device retesting remains deferred by the owner.

## 3. Plan 01: adopt, adapt, or reject

### Adopt the practical direction

1. **Network-first access.** Reuse the existing Cuelinks account before opening another operational dependency. Inspect each actual programme's access and terms. Approved network distribution is a legitimate route—not a way to ignore supplier rules.
2. **Low-complexity integrations first.** Approved links before widgets; widgets before native APIs; provider checkout before own booking/payment operations. A live-rate integration is not a prerequisite for every legitimate referral commission.
3. **Distinct India-oriented usefulness.** Family room/child costs, policy clarity and eligible bank offers are useful hypotheses. Build only on supported property/rate/offer evidence.
4. **Relevant cross-selling.** Travel essentials and destination shopping can help later, when relevant current offers and serviceability evidence exist. Do not pad the fixed goods catalogue or portray reference products as live discounts.
5. **Attribution diligence.** App hand-off, competing referrals, validation and cancellations deserve explicit checks and reconciliation.

### Reject as a foundation

- **Borrowing one provider's price to estimate another provider's price.** A common hotel name does not make supplier inventory, room terms, taxes or fees identical. Even an “estimated” number beside live rates can mislead users.
- **Automatic stacking of unconfirmed discounts.** Caps, minimum spends, exclusions, card variants, booking windows, app/member restrictions and stacking permission are independent facts.
- **A 100% incognito attribution guarantee.** Private browsing changes storage and session behaviour; it does not establish app/deep-link tracking, attribution rules or an advertiser's payout obligation.
- **Guaranteed “save 15%” direct-booking badges**, unverified mobile-rate hacks, invented delivery availability or unsupported “no OTA offers this” claims.
- **Promoting credit cards as easy money or necessary for a hotel deal.** Financial-product approval, eligibility, advertising rules and consumer suitability are not solved by an affiliate link. This is not the first hotel revenue route.
- Automatic tabs, forced clicks, hidden link replacement, click incentives, self-click tests or concealed sponsorship. These can damage user trust and invalidate revenue.

Plan 01's universal 50,000-visitor threshold, universal rejection of individuals, fixed app-loss percentages, competitor misconduct allegations and “90% of beginners” statistics were not established as universal facts and are not used in the product.

## 5. Revenue order: easiest eligible route first

### Priority A — use access already held

Review the existing Cuelinks hotel campaigns, current linked promotions and any approval conditions. A suitable existing CPS offer can be used without waiting for a native hotel API, but it earns only on the contract's qualifying event. A source link to a hotel's website does not become monetised automatically.

Do not reinstate the five previously rejected hotel promotions merely because a related campaign is open. Campaign access and an accurate, working offer are separate gates. Do not fabricate hotel-specific deep links where deep linking is prohibited.

### Priority B — investigate CPC immediately, activate only when eligible

CPC receives explicit first-review treatment in the owner desk. For each opportunity, record:

1. Exact advertiser/network and campaign identifier returned by that network.
2. Active programme and approved publisher/project status.
3. Allowed visitor/booking countries, comparison-site permission and website/PWA placement.
4. Payable event: direct outbound click, downstream click, ad impression, qualified lead or completed stay.
5. Net publisher payout basis/currency and whether it is dynamic—not EPC mistaken for commission.
6. Duplicate/bot/self-click rules, attribution window, app handling and permitted sub-IDs.
7. Validation, reversals, missing-transaction claims, payout threshold, settlement and deductions.
8. Supplied tracking route, permitted deep-link parameters and content/display rights.

A route remains blocked where a necessary fact is unknown. A live list entry is not proof that a campaign is unpaused or suitable for India.

The public Cuelinks Tripadvisor CPC page inspected in this review identifies **Germany**, says the campaign is **paused**, and describes a **dynamic** payout. It is a useful warning against treating a brand name and CPC headline as India-ready income. It does not establish the status of every Tripadvisor programme or country. [5](https://www.cuelinks.com/campaigns/tripadvisor-1-affiliate-program)

### Priority D — audience-dependent ads and direct relationships

Approved display ads may earn from ad impressions/interactions without hotel bookings, but can reduce comparison completion. Use a small, clearly separated placement only after approval and measurement—not an ad that looks like the best hotel result. Never encourage clicks to support the site.

A direct hotel's capped qualified-click or qualified-enquiry agreement is conceivable. It still needs advertiser acceptance, anti-abuse rules, reporting and payment terms. A new direct relationship is not automatically easier than using existing network access. No direct CPC, lead or fulfilled-stay agreement currently exists in the release records.

### Priority E — native API integration and expanded booking responsibility

Continue pursuing approved provider-native search in parallel with useful referrals. API implementation/certification follows actual access and terms. Keep booking fulfilment with the provider. Own payment collection, refunds, loyalty funding, financial products and a full OTA operating model remain later and more demanding—not launch shortcuts.

## 6. Better measurement: payable event before payout amount

The revised owner calculator separates:

- **Direct CPC:** plans × first-click rate × accepted-click rate × net payout per accepted click.
- **Downstream click-out share:** plans × first-click rate × partner-site onward-click rate × acceptance × the publisher's net payout per accepted downstream click.
- **CPA/direct fulfilment:** plans × action × booking/confirmation × completion × acceptance × net payout.

All deduct entered operating costs; none represents actual ledger income. Do not combine CPC and CPA for the same event unless the specific contract explicitly provides both.

**Illustration only:** 1,000 plans × 30% first-click × 20% partner-site onward-click × 90% accepted = **54 payable events**. At an invented planning input of ₹1 net per event, revenue is ₹54 before entered costs. Counting every first click as payable would overstate this scenario fivefold. These inputs are not a Tripadvisor quote, industry benchmark or forecast.

Select opportunities using **probability of approval, implementation effort, eligibility, expected validated contribution and time until cash**, not just headline commission. Track unpaid/pending/validated/paid separately. Funding promises from pending commission is not permitted.

## 7. Leakage controls and remaining gaps

| Risk | Control now / required next |
| --- | --- |
| Network access mistaken for money | Separate access, campaign type, payout basis, country and transaction evidence |
| CPC click counted twice or by the wrong definition | Separate direct and downstream models; network validation is authoritative |
| Country-ineligible campaigns | Live campaign countries shown; missing information stays unknown |
| No deep-link permission | Display returned permission; never invent hotel/date URL parameters |
| Missing-transaction recovery unavailable | Expose network reporting and missing-claim permission, do not promise recovery |
| App hand-off / last-click overwrite | Verify each campaign's published terms; no 100% attribution promise; physical testing still needed |
| Coupons invalidate attribution | Use only permitted codes and applicable restrictions; never imply copy means auto-apply |
| Paused campaign not exposed in list API | Check current campaign terms/status; list access alone cannot pass activation |
| Misleading review or price badges | Evidence required; no invented automated trust scores or cross-provider inferred prices |
| Paid traffic / operating capacity | No paid acquisition; existing free hosting/Turso retained; free limits are not unlimited capacity |
| Approval, KYC, tax and payout details | Owner-controlled facts and contractual consent remain necessary; no passwords/2FA requested |

No architecture can remove all attribution loss, cancellations, fraud, outages, third-party changes or customer choice. “Zero leakage” is not an honest release claim.
