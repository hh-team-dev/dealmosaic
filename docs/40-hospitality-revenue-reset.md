## 4. How other businesses earn—and what we should copy

### A. Referral/metasearch: outbound does not automatically mean lost revenue

Trivago reports referral revenue from advertisers, primarily CPC bids on hotel/accommodation offer clicks. Its disclosed model demonstrates that a customer can leave the website **and still generate revenue for the referring business**. It does not establish that DealMosaic has a CPC contract or can charge for its own clicks. [1](https://ir.trivago.com/static-files/5eeeede3-0d20-4841-8266-d00c95344b37)

Booking.com's official affiliate programme describes network registration and commissions on qualified bookings through integrations. That is different from publishing an ordinary search URL. [3](https://www.booking.com/affiliate-program/v2/index.html)

**Copy the mechanism:** useful matching/comparison, approved attribution and settlement reconciliation. Do not copy only the outbound button or assume every visitor pays us.

### B. Dining marketplace: partners pay for measurable business

Swiggy's official FY25 shareholder letter describes out-of-home revenue from pre-agreed restaurant commissions, advertising, user/platform/subscription fees and other services. These are commercial agreements and operating systems, not merely restaurant search results. Its scale does not establish a commission rate available to DealMosaic. [3](https://www.swiggy.com/corporate/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/Q4-FY2025-Shareholder-letter.pdf)

**Copy the mechanism:** merchant-authorised offers, fulfilled-visit evidence and agreed settlement. Start without delivery logistics, a consumer subscription or collecting the restaurant bill.

### C. Integrated OTA booking: possible, but permission-gated

Booking.com's Demand API prerequisites require Managed Affiliate Partner status, a signed contract, Partner Centre access, an API token and affiliate ID. A public developer page is not access. [1](https://developers.booking.com/demand/docs/getting-started/prerequisites)

Its accommodation tutorial explicitly distinguishes **Search, look and redirect** from **Search, look and book**; booking in our own app requires approval, a business case and an appropriate partner agreement. [1](https://developers.booking.com/demand/docs/accommodations/accommodation-tutorial)

Expedia offers Rapid API and white-label solutions, but Rapid applications are reviewed case by case. Suitability, price, approval, payment roles and support obligations must be established, not guessed. [1](https://partner.expediagroup.com/en-us/join-us/rapid-api) [1](https://partner.expediagroup.com/en-au/solutions/build-your-travel-experience)

**Copy this later**, when approval and positive unit economics justify it. Do not activate paid services, accept terms, pay deposits or submit commercial applications without the owner's authorisation.

## 5. The recommended DealMosaic model

### Core: an on-site decision and partner-offer service

Keep Hotels, Dining and Pet-friendly within the existing DealMosaic brand, account and PWA. Preserve shopping design and the warm palette. Preserve all 17 destination choices, but distinguish listed coverage from **contracted, transactable supply**. Nationwide discoverability does not mean nationwide booking capability.

The initial revenue product should be **fulfilled partner visits and stays**, not a giant catalogue. Use a limited set of cooperating restaurants and independent hotels where evidence, availability and service can actually be maintained.

| Capability | Recommended starting approach |
|---|---|
| Discovery | On-site venue/branch cards and details from lawful, sourced data. |
| Comparison | Same-date, same-room/meal, same-terms comparisons where valid inputs exist. Unknown price is unknown, not zero. |
| Direct partners | On-site offer request, merchant acceptance, reference and status. Pay the venue initially. |
| Our income | Contracted fixed fee per fulfilled visit/booking, or agreed commission on a precisely defined eligible amount. |
| Wider non-partner coverage | Useful informational listings; no fake Reserve/Book button or availability claim. |
| Affiliates | Optional authorised, labelled final provider handoff, only with valid access and permitted links. |
| Full online booking/payment | Later, only under suitable provider contracts and operational/legal readiness. |

**Why this meets the actual goal:** browsing, choosing, comparing, requesting and checking status can happen inside DealMosaic. With direct partners, no third-party website needs to open for those steps. The guest initially pays the venue; the venue owes DealMosaic its agreed fee. This retains the attributable business relationship without pretending that a new platform can safely collect every hotel's money at zero operational cost.

**If the product rule is strictly no external booking checkout, only direct partners or approved integrated-booking inventory may have a booking action. Other venues can remain useful informational listings, but we must not quietly route their primary action to another website.** Optional affiliate fulfilment is a separate, disclosed choice—not a substitute for that rule.

**Trade-off:** pay-at-venue reduces payment complexity but creates reconciliation and collection risk. It does not eliminate operating costs, legal duties, taxes or merchant acquisition work. If “everything through our website” includes payment, refunds and every chain's instant inventory, that is a later booking-platform business—not a zero-cost UI adjustment.

## 6. Why a visitor would choose us

The current generic search form and copied-code headlines are not a strong enough reason to prefer DealMosaic to a major OTA. The defensible proposition should be:

**Find a suitable place, understand what you will actually pay, and obtain a benefit whose conditions and fulfilment route are clear.**

- Genuine partner-funded benefits, when contracted: an off-peak set menu, breakfast inclusion, agreed late checkout or another useful benefit. Never manufacture a higher reference price or promise a conditional upgrade as guaranteed.
- Explicit suitability: vegetarian options versus a fully vegetarian kitchen, accessible facilities only when evidenced, family/group needs, neighbourhood and travel constraints, and branch-specific pet permissions.
- Transparent total: taxes and mandatory charges, refund/cancellation terms, membership and payment requirements. Cashback remains separate from today's payable price.
- A shortlist and comparison people can understand, plus reminders only where consent and delivery actually exist.
- Specific, sourced local guidance rather than thin city pages. Pet travel remains a differentiated collection within this system—not a claim that every pet can enter every restaurant.
- Less uncertainty: clear distinctions between public information, reviewed policy, contracted partner, merchant-accepted request and confirmed reservation.

We cannot credibly promise universally lower prices than Booking.com, Agoda, MakeMyTrip or any other provider without equivalent evidence. Better suitability, honest conditions and genuine partner benefits can be the reason to return. If those cannot be delivered, more design alone is not a viable revenue strategy.

## 7. The redesigned customer experience

### Dining

**Destination / optional area → on-site results → branch details → compare or save → eligible partner offer → request → merchant acceptance → visit → fulfilment confirmation.**

- Category controls update native results, rather than immediately opening Google Maps.
- Cards show branch identity, cuisine, sourced dietary information, evidence date and applicable benefit. No invented ratings, opening status or average spend.
- Filters include restaurants, vegetarian options, cafés/bakeries and fast food; do not call places “around you” without an explicit location choice/consent and a supported distance calculation. The current category is “Local restaurants”, not a verified non-vegetarian-only list.
- Venue detail shows terms, exclusions, supported service modes and clearly differentiated informational versus partner actions.
- “Request a table” appears only where a real operator can receive and fulfil the request. It must not read “Confirmed” until the merchant accepts and provides a confirmation.
- Directions, official sites and source citations remain optional external actions. Do not hide the destination or pretend their content is ours.

### Hotels

**Destination + dates + guests → on-site suitable property results → compare valid offers/quotes → full terms → partner request or approved booking flow → confirmation/status.**

- Do not show fabricated live rates. Licensed live availability, documented partner allotment or a timestamped manual quote determines the status.
- Compare room category, occupancy, meals, cancellation and mandatory charges—not unlike products under a single “cheapest” badge.
- General external searches become secondary tools, not the primary results experience or a purported revenue path.
- Approved affiliate fulfilment remains visibly labelled. Without that optional handoff, transactable coverage is limited to direct partners or an approved integrated-booking provider.
- Browser reload/resizing must not create or repeat a booking. Network mutations require idempotency and explicit confirmation.

### Design rules

Use the existing hospitality visual language; no black/blue palette change or shopping redesign. Maintain one five-item dock and the fixed install/deals bar. Reserve adequate space above the dock for primary actions, respect keyboard/safe-area changes, use visible loading/error/retry states, and provide a real return/recovery action. A new tab is not our conversion strategy; an iframe is not our booking engine.

## 8. Data and rights: native listing without copying Google

We can render our own venue records and lawful content inside DealMosaic. We must not scrape Google reviews/photos into a clone or assume a free Maps search URL grants database rights. Google's documentation restricts prefetching/storage of Maps content except specified cases such as place IDs. [3](https://developers.google.com/maps/optimize-web-services)

Preferred sources, in order:

1. Merchant-supplied details, menus and photos with recorded publication permission and change responsibility.
2. Carefully researched public facts and official links, with our own summaries and review dates; do not bulk-copy protected descriptions or images.
3. Properly licensed open geographic data. OpenStreetMap permits commercial use under ODbL; attribution, licence obligations and infrastructure usage policies still apply. “Open data” is not unlimited free tile/geocoding service. [3](https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/FAQ)
4. Contracted inventory/content APIs when access, caching/display rights and economics are known.

Every record needs stable venue/branch identity, provenance, permission scope, source and review time, and independent state fields for policy, price, availability, partnership and offer validity. Search-index evidence is not the same as contacting a business. Do not merge similarly named branches or infer admission from generic tags.

## 9. Revenue operating loop and controls

For direct partners, agree in writing: legal entity and branch, authorised contact, content rights, offer funding, qualifying event, cancellation/no-show treatment, discount exclusions, commission base, taxes where applicable, payment timetable, dispute evidence and exit terms.

Proposed fulfilment state machine:

**Requested → accepted or declined → confirmed (where a reservation is actually made) → fulfilled or cancelled/no-show → commission pending → validated/disputed → invoiced/payable → paid.**

- A request is not a reservation; a reservation is not a completed visit; a completed visit is not automatically paid commission.
- Use a unique request/redemption reference and authenticated, branch-scoped merchant confirmation. A QR code alone is not proof of fulfilment.
- Rate-limit requests, prevent duplicate redemption and use idempotent transitions. Restrict each merchant to its own records.
- Reconcile merchant proof with guest acknowledgement or a minimal redacted receipt when necessary. Do not collect full payment-card details.
- Invoice or reconcile agreed fees; manual settlement must be marked paid only after actual confirmation. No automatic financial action is authorised by this plan.
- Separate sponsored placements from objective comparisons. Sponsors do not buy an undisclosed ranking advantage.
- Suppress stale or reported-broken offers pending review. A 200 response, safe URL or future feed expiry is not proof the journey works.
- Separate network-reported pending/validated/payable/paid from platform click counts. Do not make internal test traffic look like customer conversions.

Existing goods-commerce rules remain separate. The owner being the sole seller/payee for handcrafted goods does **not** make them the supplier of a hotel's room or a restaurant's meal. Indian tax, consumer, privacy and payment obligations require role-specific review before marketplace/payment activation. This plan does not introduce a GSTIN field into ordinary product entry.

## 10. Unit economics—not an income promise

No commission, exclusivity or merchant interest is assumed. The following is an **illustrative negotiation model**, not market research, a quote or a forecast:

- 1,000 qualified dining sessions in a period.
- 5% submit a partner request: 50.
- 60% accepted: 30.
- 80% of accepted requests fulfilled: 24.
- Contracted fee assumed at ₹60 per fulfilled visit: **₹1,440 gross commission before costs, taxes and collection losses**.
- If only 90% of that amount is collected in the period: **₹1,296 collected**, not ₹1,440 cash.

This illustrates why more listings, bigger discounts and more clicks do not by themselves create meaningful income. Fees must cover support, merchant maintenance, disputes and acquisition. Discounts should be genuinely merchant-funded unless separately budgeted; a platform-funded discount can exceed the commission and make each conversion loss-making.

Track qualified visits, native result engagement, detail views, requests, merchant acceptance time, fulfilled visits, approved affiliate conversions, validated commission, collected cash, reversals and variable costs. Separate traffic volume from conversion and distinguish gross commission from profit.

A sensible first commercial milestone is **one real, fulfilled transaction with a documented fee actually collected**, followed by repeatability across a small set of partners—not a large revenue number on a dashboard.

## 11. Implementation order and release gates

### Phase 0 — Trust and reliability

**Already done:** evidence review, fresh targeted affiliate-access check, nine own-site mode checks, direct provider diagnostics, five-row reversible moderation and live API verification.

**Next build:** shared offer registry/health states; canonical duplicate suppression; separate booking/stay date windows with explicit timezone; branch and payment eligibility; branded unavailable/recovery page; report-a-link mechanism; safe fallback and permission-aware destination handling. Fresh network access should supersede stale campaign cache without implying a booking occurred.

**Gate:** no known contradictory or broken promoted route; browser-mode/return tests on our routes; semantic provider checks where permitted; exact user's iQOO journey assessed separately. Do not bypass anti-bot checks or auto-click affiliate links to simulate success.

### Phase 1 — Native discovery and comparison

Replace general Maps-launch category tiles with native filtering and venue details. Preserve all 17 city choices, use sourced records without padding, and expose honest gaps. Add shortlist/comparison with clear persistence/consent controls. Keep maps/sources as optional tools and preserve existing pet-directory evidence distinctions.

**Gate:** actual searchable records, accessible phone results, no invented prices/reviews/partners, proper source/content rights, no dead primary action, website/PWA/classic parity and current handover.

### Phase 2 — Direct-partner pilot

Prepare a merchant information sheet, commercial-terms checklist, offer intake and fulfilment flow. Select two pilot destinations based on actual traffic, merchant responsiveness and operational feasibility—not merely the largest city or an assumed personal contact network. Suggested recruitment goal: 5–10 restaurants and 3–5 independent stays; this is a target, not present supply. Keep broader discovery available while transactable coverage is clearly limited.

Build the merchant/admin controls and request/reference lifecycle privately before activation. Do not put a live request form in front of users without contracted recipients, support capacity and a fulfilment process.

**Gate:** signed permissions/terms, verified branch/contact, supported availability workflow, privacy/retention and abuse controls, cancellation/dispute process, plus explicit owner approval for outreach and contracts. No unsolicited applications or merchant messages have been sent in this pass.

### Phase 3 — Demonstrate collection

Run authorised real business, reconcile fulfilment and fees, and measure time/cost per transaction. Expand only when at least one settlement has actually been collected and repeated fulfilments show sustainable contribution. Do not use production test bookings, fake redemptions or owner-funded purchases to manufacture evidence.

### Phase 4 — Approved inventory/booking integration

Only after suitable agreements and economics are established, connect authorised APIs or white-label booking, implement booking/payment/cancellation responsibilities, and pass the supplier's requirements. Do not promise a price, entitlement or launch date before commercial acceptance. There is no guaranteed all-in-one, all-provider, zero-cost integration available to us merely because APIs are documented.
