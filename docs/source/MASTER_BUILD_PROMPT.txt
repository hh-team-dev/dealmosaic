MASTER BUILD PROMPT — DEALMOSAIC

A colourful, intelligent, global product-price comparison platform

Act as an entire senior product-development team consisting of:

- A principal product strategist.
- A global e-commerce and price-comparison researcher.
- A senior UI/UX and visual interaction designer.
- A full-stack software architect.
- A frontend and backend engineering team.
- A database and API integration engineer.
- An affiliate-marketing and monetisation specialist.
- A cybersecurity and privacy specialist.
- A QA and performance-testing engineer.

Your assignment is to research, architect, design and BUILD a complete, functional, responsive website called DEALMOSAIC.

Working brand tagline:

ONE SEARCH. THREE REAL DEALS. ZERO PRICE SURPRISES.

The name is provisional. Keep branding configurable and check domain and trademark availability before recommending it as a final commercial name.

This must be a genuine product-price comparison platform, not an ordinary shopping website, not an online marketplace, and not a static catalogue.

Users search for a product. The platform searches all authorised and connected shopping sources available for the selected country, identifies the same product across retailers, verifies available price information, calculates comparable costs, and presents the three strongest available offers side by side.

The platform must initially be designed to operate using free infrastructure and authorised free data sources wherever possible.

IMPORTANT: Do not claim that all global retailers can be searched in real time for free. Do not invent APIs, retailer partnerships, prices, product reviews or cashback arrangements. Build a modular system that can expand as genuine integrations become available.

PART 1 — PRODUCT VISION

Build an online destination where consumers can compare practically anything they might purchase online.

Examples include:

- Smartphones and electronics.
- Computers and accessories.
- Clothing and footwear.
- Beauty and personal care.
- Home appliances and furniture.
- Kitchen products.
- Books.
- Sports and fitness equipment.
- Toys.
- Groceries, where comparable product data is available.
- Other physical consumer products.

The user should be able to type something as simple as:

"Samsung Galaxy S25 Ultra 512GB"

The platform should identify the exact product and find matching offers from connected retailers.

The output should be a maximum of three leading offers, preferably side by side on desktop and as elegant responsive cards on mobile.

A user must be able to understand within seconds:

1. Which retailer has the lowest verified total price?
2. How much will the product actually cost?
3. What additional charges are known?
4. Is the seller reliable?
5. How many product reviews are available?
6. How quickly can the product arrive?
7. What is the return policy?
8. When was the price last checked?
9. Why has this offer been ranked first, second or third?

The platform does not process payments or sell products itself. Purchases happen directly on the retailer's website through properly attributed links where permitted.

Ranking logic

The default ranking must be based on verified landed cost.

1. Lowest confirmed total payable price.
