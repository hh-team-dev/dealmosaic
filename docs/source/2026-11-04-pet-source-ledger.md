# Pet directory — source review ledger, 4 November 2026

## Scope and evidence

41 unique listed entries: 23 stays and 18 dining places. This is a curated, non-exhaustive directory, not a current-admission, comprehensive-inventory, operating-status or checkout-price certification. The 17 destination choices include overlapping Delhi NCR, Gurugram and Noida. Restaurant evidence remains insufficient for Haridwar and Jamshedpur; this is a research gap, not a claim that no venues exist.

`official` identifies the publisher, not independent verification. `page` means the relevant page text was fetched during research; `index` means the relevant text was available through the search index only. A source review date is not a direct venue-confirmation date. Hotel admission does not imply restaurant admission. Dog/cat mentions are included only when explicit; by-request cats do not enter the explicit-cat filter. Unspecified pets do not establish bird, rabbit or other-species permission. Public CSV and UI use the same JSON records. Descriptions below are factual summaries, not copied reviews or star ratings.

Primary data: `apps/web/src/data/pet-venues.json`. Exact source URLs, publishers, evidence methods, limitations and conflicts are carried into the UI and CSV. Search-result reference numbers are local to their research calls, not globally unique identifiers. Read the cited original before relying on a condition.

## Deliberate exclusions and offer decisions

- A generic pet-friendly category filter is not property-specific evidence; resident-animal cafés do not establish permission to bring a visitor's own pet.
- Contradictory property evidence, closed-place reports, weak old anecdotes and automated itinerary prose were not used to fill gaps. Examples rejected during review include ibis Gurgaon (official negative versus third-party positive), Twisty Tails (outside pets not permitted), Dori Dhan Mill and Boho Noida (closed reports), and a 2020 Brubeck/Jamshedpur anecdote without adequate current branch evidence.
- Marriott's historical pet staycation offer, [1](https://www.marriott.com/offers/pet-staycation-package-off-47825), carried 2022 validity and is not a current offer. Hyatt Gachibowli's older indexed fee-waiver news redirected to unrelated current content when fetched; it did not establish an eligible present-day offer.
- No date-checked, named-venue-matched offer with documented pet eligibility was established. `pet-offers.json` intentionally contains an empty array. This does not mean none exists. General room-rate promotions may qualify later if the exact property, eligible pet room/visit, validity and terms are established; they need not waive the pet fee. Generic brand-level travel coupons alone are insufficient.
- No merchant was contacted. No admission, room availability, live bill, coupon redemption or transaction was independently checked.
