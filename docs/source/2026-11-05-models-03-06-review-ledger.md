## Model05 — broad feature ideas; highest-risk shortcuts

- 001–006,015–020,025–029: comparison layers, inclusion checks, whole-trip costs and seasonality. Keep customer problem framing; existing exact groups are stronger. No arbitrary authenticity score, 'zero complaints' guarantee, fake historical price data or unsupported festival savings.
- 007,018–025: **Reject automatic coupon + bank + app + cashback stacking**, provider-A price ×1.02 presented as provider-B price, 12%/18% GST formula and default 10% service charge. An 'estimated' label does not legitimise invented supplier rates. Cashback is not payable-price reduction. Deep-link permission must be actual, not assumed.
- 008–011: refund-speed meter, festival predictor, duration optimisation, split stays. Useful questions, **not launchable factual ratings/optimisers without comparable authorised data**. Complaints are biased samples, not measured refund medians. Extra nights can lower per-night price while increasing total expenditure. Split bookings can require room moves, separate deposits, interrupted cancellation rights and double booking; do not recommend automatically. Rebooking cash-flow tool addresses part of this problem safely.
- 012–014,021–024,030–032,045–047: account/API/data setup. No universal free hotel API or unrestricted Google content licence. No stack migration, speculative serviceability, invented shopping codes, incognito attribution promise, or 6–24-hour price cache irrespective of partner terms.
- 033–039,055–064: helpful original content/community idea, but reject arbitrary 100-page/traffic targets, unaudited competitor X/check tables, automatic price-drop pages, unfunded ₹50 UPI cashback, fake exclusives and claims of earnings on every interaction. Shared site identity and accounts already exist.
- 040–044: **Material tax errors**: $250,000 LRS is an outward-remittance facility, not an inbound affiliate-income cap; sole proprietorship does not itself exempt a person from state profession tax; universal 10% TDS and simple ₹20 lakh exemption are not owner-specific advice. Do not hard-code rates or deductions. Obtain applicable tax/payout classification from the network and qualified adviser.
- 048–054: rail/temple/weekend/group demand ideas can guide later research; no assumed IRCTC affiliate access, blanket market percentages, verified local-ID acceptance or 'chaotic hotel' allegations. Do not expand beyond the requested 17 destinations on illustrative numbers.

## Model06 — useful corrections and narrower scope

- 001–003,014–016: explicitly retracts 100% incognito protection, unchecked card schedules, assumed campaign coverage and revenue benchmarks. Keep. 'Public' review counts still require lawful reuse; disclaimers do not cure an unsupported supplier price.
- 003–008,012–014,017–019: decision support, neutral sourced notes, focused needs, group requests and essentials. Adopt generic need checklist and relevant navigation; defer public checkout reports and lead submissions until privacy/moderation/supply response exist. Dropping statistical outliers must not erase genuine complaints or create a misleading average. No uniqueness guarantee or claim that moderation costs nothing.
- 008–010: sensible conditional sequence—approved affiliates, evidence-backed paid leads, labelled sponsorship, then ads/regulatory-heavy referrals. Preserve owner's CPC preference while executing the easiest permitted route. Zero revenue remains possible.
- 011–013: foundations/security/mobile/genuine content useful, but already largely implemented. No paid domain; no requirement to publish owner photo just to rank. Grievance/intermediary duties depend on actual activity; a disclaimer does not provide legal immunity.
- 015–017: no dark patterns, minimal data, phased privacy rules, tax review, diversification and 1–2 hours/day upkeep. Acknowledge workload: that is precisely why unstaffed RFQs and public reports are not zero-fuss additions.

Existing price-comparison and commercial gates remain unchanged. Test and deployment status must be recorded separately; this ledger alone does not assert release completion.
